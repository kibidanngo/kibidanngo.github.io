Ryota Takaki

Biological Soft Matter Physics

Postdoctoral researcher: Max Planck Institute for the Physics of Complex Systems

I am fascinated by the out-of-equilibrium phenomena in Biology. My research focuses on formulating theoretical frameworks to understand the non-equilibrium phenomena using the principles of statistical physics.

My current research interests lie in the non-equilibrium dynamics of biological condensates and genome organization.
